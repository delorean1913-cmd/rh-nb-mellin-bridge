# Reference ecosystem

This file lists the intended proof ecosystem. Do not upload copyrighted PDFs unless you have redistribution rights.

## Core NB/BD references

- A. Beurling, closure problem related to the Riemann zeta-function.
- B. Nyman, one-dimensional translation group and semigroup in certain function spaces.
- L. Báez-Duarte, strengthening of the Nyman--Beurling criterion.
- M. Balazard, E. Saias, and M. Yor, notes on the Nyman--Beurling equivalent of RH.

## Analytic number theory tools

- E. C. Titchmarsh, revised by D. R. Heath-Brown, *The Theory of the Riemann Zeta-Function*.
- H. Iwaniec and E. Kowalski, *Analytic Number Theory*.
- H. L. Montgomery and R. C. Vaughan, multiplicative number theory and Hilbert/large sieve methods.
- J. B. Conrey and collaborators on mollifiers and zeta moments.
- D. Goldston, H. Montgomery, K. Soundararajan, and related work on pair correlation and primes in short intervals.

## Project-specific rule

Citations in public posts should point to stable public bibliographic pages, DOIs, arXiv pages, journal pages, or books. Avoid linking to local uploaded scans.
