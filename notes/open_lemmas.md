# Open lemmas

## Lemma A: Compact critical-line approximation

Layer: conjectural \(L^2_{\rm eff}\) bridge

Claim to test: for fixed \(T_0\), the smoothed Möbius approximants are sufficient to make the weighted low-range contribution tend to zero.

Failure mode: critical-line zeros create a local obstruction that requires a different approximant.

## Lemma B: Medium-range mollifier estimate

Layer: conjectural \(L^2_{\rm eff}\) bridge

Claim to test: for a growing \(T_N\),

```math
\int_{T_0<|t|\le T_N}\left|\zeta(1/2+it)P_N(1/2+it)-1\right|^2{dt\over 1/4+t^2}=o(1).
```

Failure mode: known mollifier estimates do not allow the required polynomial length or uniformity.

## Lemma C: Tail decay

Layer: conjectural \(L^2_{\rm eff}\) bridge

Claim to test: the denominator and smoothing yield

```math
\int_{|t|>T_N}\left|\zeta(1/2+it)P_N(1/2+it)-1\right|^2{dt\over 1/4+t^2}=o(1).
```

Failure mode: the Dirichlet polynomial term has too much mean-square mass in the tail.
